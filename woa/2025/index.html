NATIONAL DAY OF ACTION FOR MEDICAID on SEPTEMBER 6 2025!

Every year since 2020, the Nonviolent Medicaid Army has hosted a national Week of Action in September. In 2025, the NVMA organized a coordinated National Day of Action for Medicaid on September 6. Each participating state was asked to hold a rally/speakout, big or small, consisting of speakers, songs, education, and most importantly, people lifting up their own stories and experiences, describing the plight (impact), fight (the power of our collective struggle) and insight (what we’re learning).

For more information and to get involved contact: nonviolentmedicaidarmy@gmail.com
